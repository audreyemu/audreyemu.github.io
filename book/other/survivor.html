Survivor @ UCLA

I have watched every season of Survivor since Season 32 Kaoh Rong in 2016. My parents were fans of the show and we would watch it together every Wednesday. At the time, I only cared about the challenges and would have my parents call me out of my room whenever a challenge came on.

But as I’ve grown, I’ve realized the challenges are the boring part. The really intriguing part of Survivor is the social dynamics it explores. You need to fit in well, but not so well that you’re considered a threat. You need to lie to people, while keeping yourself on their good side. It’s a game full of contradictions and I can’t get enough.

One day, when I was studying at Young Research Library at UCLA, I took a bathroom break and noticed a flyer on the stall door. It was promoting a club called Survivor @ UCLA. They plan, film, and produce their own mini version of the show – complete with challenges, advantages, blindsides, and one sole survivor. After some encouragement from my friends, I sent in an application to be a contestant and was accepted!

I competed on Season 5 of Survivor @ UCLA and had the most fun and stressful quarter of my life. It was really difficult getting blindsided and lying to people, but we had to remember that this was just a game and it was all in good fun.

I was able to win challenges, play an idol, and make it to the finale. My puzzle skills, loyal reputation, and social adaptation carried me through the season. My main focus was on managing my threat level – making people think of me as a number instead of a threat. I also set up lots of 1:1 talks with people, making deeper connections and earning more trust. I always ensured there was at least 1 bigger threat in front of me up until the last vote, where I could be the frontrunner going into the finale. I won’t spoil how the season ends.

Season 5 is scheduled to be uploaded to Survivor @ UCLA’s youtube channel in 2025/6, but you can view this sneak peak of episode 1: Survivor @ UCLA: One World - Episode 1

You can find more information at this website: Survivor @ UCLA - Season 5 and find edited episodes here: YouTube - Survivor UCLA

It’s a really unique club and I am very grateful to have found it during my time at UCLA. We’ll see if it prepares me for maybe competing on the real show someday!
